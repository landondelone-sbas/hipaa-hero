HOW TO PLAY

PURPOSE

This is your annual HIPAA compliance refresher. It covers the Privacy Rule, the Security Rule, the Breach Notification Rule, and our clinic's own reporting procedures — the material an OCR auditor expects every workforce member to know.

THE RUN

  • TRAINING MODULES — 10 modules of learning cards, each ending in a short mini quiz. Score 80% to clear a module.
  • PRACTICE QUIZ — 10 random questions with instant explanations. Unlimited retries, not recorded as your official result.
  • FINAL EXAM — 30 questions drawn from all categories. Explanations are withheld until you finish.

SCORING

  • 100 points per correct answer, plus a speed bonus for answering quickly.
  • Consecutive correct answers build a combo multiplier up to x3.
  • Select-all-that-apply questions are all-or-nothing — no partial credit.
  • Passing score: 80%.
  • Questions and answer options are randomized on every attempt.

CONTROLS

  • Click an answer, or press its letter key A–D.
  • Press Enter to submit, then Enter again to advance.
  • Everything is keyboard navigable. Motion can be disabled in Settings.

Estimated time: about 18 minutes for the exam, longer if you work through all modules first. Question bank: 55 questions. Your progress is stored only in this browser — nothing is uploaded.
